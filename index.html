MEDICAL AI & COMPUTER VISION

Connecting evidence.

I'm currently a Direct Ph.D. Student at 3DV Lab in the School of Computer Science and Technology, Hangzhou Dianzi University, and fortunate to be advised by Prof. Feiwei Qin（秦飞巍）.

My joint research training is with BIRTHLab in the School of Biomedical Engineering at Tsinghua University, under the co-supervision of Prof. Qiyuan Tian（田启源）.

My research explores how visual and multimodal representations can support medical image understanding—from lesion grounding in 3D CT and computational pathology to robust segmentation with missing modalities.
